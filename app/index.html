Roadmap to the platinum

About 200 hours, one playthrough, difficulty 4/10 (PowerPyx). Cheats disable trophies and saving, so keep them off. There is no mission replay, so keep a save at the start and end of each chapter. The only trophy that can be missed is flagged below.

      Missable trophies

      Only one trophy in the whole list is reported as possibly missable: Positive Mental Attitude — the Chapter 1 meditation at Jason’s house. After the mission Down to Earth, talk to Lucia in the kitchen in the morning, follow her to the balcony and accept when she offers to show you how to meditate. Everything else, story trophies included, can be earned after the story in free-roam. Source: PowerPyx — GTA 6 Trophy Guide & Roadmap.
